About Smart Expense Tracker

Smart Expense Tracker is a simple web application designed to help users manage their personal finances.

With this tool you can easily record your income and expenses, view your balance, and understand your spending habits.

Features include:

  • Track income and expenses
  • Visual chart reports
  • Monthly balance calculation
  • Dark mode support
  • Mobile responsive design
